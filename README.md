# Jarvis Labs

Jarvis Labs is an experimental Human–AI systems lab exploring how humans and increasingly autonomous AI systems should share intelligence, control, and decision-making.

Rather than asking only *what can AI automate?*, we study *what should AI automate, when should it defer back to the human, and what happens to human capability when it does not?*

Our work combines **operational system design**—identifying where AI can take over tasks—with **behavioral system design**—identifying where automation creates risks such as overreliance, skill degradation, sycophancy, poor calibration, or loss of human agency.

We build and experimentally test new interaction models for human–AI collaboration: adaptive delegation, trust and calibration mechanisms, behavioral nudges, control over agent decision trees, reciprocal human–AI feedback, multi-agent systems, and interfaces that change behavior depending on the relative capabilities of the human and the AI.

Jarvis Labs will continue within **Sundai** as an evolving research and prototyping effort. The repository will grow with new applications, experiments, measurement tools, datasets, and system prototypes, with selected results developed into open reports and original research publications.

The long-term goal is to understand—and engineer—the emerging boundary between **human judgment and machine autonomy**, so that AI expands what humans can do without quietly replacing the capabilities we still need.
